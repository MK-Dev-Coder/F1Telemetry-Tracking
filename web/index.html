How it works

Three ways in

Replay real F1 races, or see your own laps from the game.

  1. Pick a race, 2022–2026
  2. Press Play or go lap by lap
  3. Click a driver to see why
  • Pit stop chanceEvery driver, next 3 laps. BOX = likely to stop.
  • Next tyreThe tyre each driver would most likely fit.
  • Race outcomeWin, podium and points chances, lap by lap.
  • EngineerTyre life, strategies and a race prediction.

Data from FastF1 and OpenF1. The 2026 races weren't used for training, so they're a fair test.

  1. Settings → Telemetry Settings
  2. UDP On, port 20777
  3. Broadcast On, format 2025
  • Live timingLeaderboard, laps, tyres and events.
  • Pit strategyThe real-F1 model, live on your race.
  • Team radioHold T and ask: "Should I box?"
  • TelemetryCompare two laps, find the lost time.
