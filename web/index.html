F1 rules

How a race works

The rules you need to follow a race, and the strategy calls in this app.

Points in a Grand Prix

P125P218P315 P412P510P68 P76P84P92 P101
  • PracticeTeams try set-ups and tyres. No points.
  • QualifyingThree knockout rounds. The fastest lap in Q3 takes pole.
  • The raceAbout 305 km, at most 2 hours. Points for the top 10.
  • Sprint weekendsSix a year add a short Saturday race: points for the top 8.

Since 2025 there's no extra point for the fastest lap.

SSoftMMedium HHardIInter WWet
  • Softer is fasterSoft has the most grip but wears out first. Hard lasts longest.
  • Two dry tyresIn a dry race everyone must use two different dry tyres, so everyone stops.
  • Rain tyresInters for a damp track, wets for heavy rain. Then the two-tyre rule doesn't apply.
  • Tyre ageOld tyres lose time every lap: that's when the pit model's chance goes up.
  • Pit stopNew tyres take 2–3 s, but the whole stop costs around 20 s.
  • UndercutPit first: new tyres are faster, so you come out ahead of the car you followed.
  • OvercutStay out longer and gain while the car that pitted warms up its new tyres.
  • Safety car = cheap stopUnder a safety car or VSC everyone is slow, so a stop costs much less.

Penalties: 5 or 10 seconds, served at the next stop or added at the end. Worse ones mean a drive-through or a stop-and-go.

  • GreenTrack clear: racing again.
  • YellowDanger ahead: slow down, no overtaking.
  • RedSession stopped: cars wait in the pit lane.
  • BlueA faster car is lapping you: let it by.
  • Black and whiteA warning, e.g. for track limits.
  • ChequeredThe end of the session.
  • Safety carThe field queues behind it: no overtaking.
  • Virtual safety carEveryone slows to a set speed.
  • Active aeroMoving wings: low drag on straights, more grip in corners.
  • Overtake modeReplaces DRS: within 1 s of the car ahead you get extra electric power.
  • Half electricAbout half the power comes from the battery, on 100% sustainable fuel.
  • New grid11 teams and 22 cars: Cadillac joins and Sauber becomes Audi.

The cars are also smaller and about 30 kg lighter. The F1 26 section shows active aero and overtake mode lap by lap.
