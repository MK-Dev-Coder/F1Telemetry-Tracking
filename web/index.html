How this app works

Three ways in: real Formula 1 races with a pit stop prediction model, or your own sessions from F1 25 and from its 2026 Season Pack (F1 26).

F1: real races

  • Pick any race from 2022 to 2026, then step through it lap by lap or press Play to watch it as if it were live.
  • Every lap, the model gives each driver's chance of a pit stop in the next 3 laps. BOX marks the drivers likely to stop.
  • Click a driver to see why: the reasons come from SHAP, in plain English, such as "tyres are 30 laps old".
  • It also picks the tyre they would most likely fit. Tick Show what happened to compare with what really happened.
  • Engineer: the briefing for the race's track, from only the races there before it: how long each tyre lasts on a day this hot, the strategies and winners there, and the race prediction.
  • Leaderboard, Pit strategy, Driver tracker (track map and head-to-head) and Race control, like the F1 TV timing screen.
  • The data comes from FastF1 (2022) and OpenF1 (2023–2026). The 2026 races were not used to train the model, so they are a fair test.

F1 25: your game sessions

  • F1 25 on your PS5 sends telemetry to this PC while you drive: Settings → Telemetry Settings, UDP Telemetry On, Broadcast Mode On (or this PC's address), port 20777, format 2025.
  • Every session is recorded by itself and shows up live: the leaderboard, your lap and sector times, tyres, events and the final result.
  • Pit strategy in races: the same pit stop model, live, with its reasons and the tyre each car would fit. It shows the model working live; the game's AI plans its own strategy.
  • Engineer: the game's own plan (the pit window from its strategy screen, where you'd rejoin, how long it says each tyre lasts, your tyre sets) beside real F1 at that track, scaled to your race's length.
  • Team radio: hold T and ask your engineer ("how are my tyres?", "should I box?"); the answer is spoken back. A free local AI (Ollama) words the answers from your live data; without it the engineer answers from the data.
  • Telemetry: speed, throttle, brake and gear along the lap. Compare any two laps and see where you gained or lost time.
  • Track map drawn from your own driving, and your car: tyre wear and temperatures, fuel, ERS and damage.
  • Sessions are kept on this PC, so you can open any of them again later.

F1 26: the 2026 Season Pack

  • Your sessions with the 2026 cars and teams (Audi and Cadillac too) get their own section, with everything the F1 25 section has.
  • Set the game's UDP Format to 2026: the app then also shows active aero (straight and corner mode) and overtake mode along each lap, OT on the leaderboard while a car uses it, and the energy you may harvest per lap.
  • Sessions with the 2025 cars stay in the F1 25 section, whichever format the game sends.

Good to know

  • Start the app by double-clicking start_app.bat, and keep its window open while you use it.
  • Everything stays on this PC: nothing is uploaded anywhere.
