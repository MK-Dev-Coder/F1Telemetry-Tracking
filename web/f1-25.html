Live demo: the pit stop and tyre models trained on real F1 races (2022–2025) get your game's laps, turned into the same features by the same code. In a shorter race the game wears the tyres out faster, so the lap counts are scaled to the real race at that track. The game's AI plans its own strategy, so this shows the models working live during a race, not how accurate they are (that comes from the real 2026 races).

Move over a chart to read the values at that point of the lap.
The track is drawn from your own driving; positions come straight from the game.
